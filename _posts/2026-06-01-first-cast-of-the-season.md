Ice finally off the bay, and the walleye didn't waste any time letting us know they were still around.

Got out right at first light. Water was still cold enough that the bite didn't turn on until mid-morning, but once it did, it didn't let up. A good reminder that patience beats an early alarm most days.

## Gear notes

- Jigs worked better than live bait once the sun got higher
- Need to re-spool the second reel before the next trip
